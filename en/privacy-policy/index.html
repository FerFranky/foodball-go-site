Foodball Go privacy policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026

Summary

Foodball Go is a mobile game developed by FerFranky. The app does not have user accounts or its own server for storing profiles, matches, or images. This policy explains what information may be processed when you use the game and its rewarded ads.

Information processed by Google Mobile Ads

Foodball Go integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show rewarded ads. Depending on your device settings, region, and applicable consent choices, Google may process:

  • IP address and approximate network or location data;
  • device or app identifiers;
  • product interactions, such as ad requests and views;
  • diagnostic, performance, and fraud information; and
  • other technical data or identifiers that Google Mobile Ads needs to operate, measure, and protect its services.

Google may collect or share this data to deliver, measure, limit, personalize—where applicable—and protect ads. Foodball Go does not receive or control all data processed by Google. See the Google Privacy Policy and information about how Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services.

Information stored locally

The game stores the preferences and progress it needs to work on your device, such as audio and language settings, progress, tournaments, and Football Album unlocks. This data stays in the app's private storage and is not sent to a Foodball Go server.

You can remove this data in Android settings by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it. This may erase your local progress.

Image export

Football Album lets you export images of your unlocks. When you choose to do so, Foodball Go asks Android to save the image to the Downloads folder through MediaStore. You initiate the export; the image is not uploaded to a Foodball Go server. You can delete exported images from your device's file manager or gallery.

Sharing, security, and retention

Foodball Go does not sell personal information or run its own user database. Information processed by Google Mobile Ads is subject to Google's practices and policies. Network connections used by the SDK are protected by Android and Google's transport security.

Local data is kept until you remove it, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. Google determines retention of the data it processes through its services according to its policies.

Children's privacy

Foodball Go does not ask for names, email addresses, contacts, camera, microphone, or precise location. Target-audience classification and Google Play declarations are completed separately in Play Console. Parents and guardians should review the advertising and consent choices available on the device and in Google Play.

Contact and changes

For questions about this policy or Foodball Go privacy, open an issue on the Foodball Go public site.

We may update this policy when the game, its SDKs, or legal requirements change. We will publish the current version at this same URL.
